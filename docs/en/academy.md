# Cogneon Akademie

The Cogneon Akademie is a place for learning and exchange [in Nuremberg](/en/contact/). In a broader sense, it also stands for the community of everyone who learns with us in projects, events and networks.

The **Cogneon Akademie** was founded in summer 2011, when we moved from Erlangen to the [Rilkepark](https://www.rilkepark.de/) in Nuremberg. Its role model and namesake was the [Platonic Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_Academy) of ancient Greece, with its astonishingly modern learning principles.

## Rooms

- **[Kitchen](https://wiki.cogneon.de/K%C3%BCche_(Cogneon_Akademie))** – fully equipped kitchen for cooking and eating together
- **[Living room](https://wiki.cogneon.de/Plenum_(Cogneon_Akademie))** – the main room of the academy for coworking, workshops, project meetings and trainings
- **[Studio](https://wiki.cogneon.de/Plenum_(Cogneon_Akademie))** – two workstations equipped for audio and video production
- **[La Boca](https://wiki.cogneon.de/La_Boca_(Cogneon_Akademie))** – workshop room with whiteboards all around, in the colors of the [La Boca neighborhood in Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Boca)
- **[Library](https://wiki.cogneon.de/Bibliothek_(Cogneon_Akademie))** – technology-free room for conversations, meetings and workshops, with many books and media
