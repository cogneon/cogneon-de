# Contact

## Intro call { #erstgespraech }

In an intro call we clarify your situation and suggest a suitable format. 30 minutes, free of charge. Briefly tell us what it is about and we will get back to you with possible dates.

[Request an intro call by email](mailto:info@cogneon.de?subject=Intro%20call){ .cg-button }

For anything else, you can reach us at [info@cogneon.de](mailto:info@cogneon.de?subject=Contact%20via%20cogneon.de) or by phone at +49 911 39570830.
