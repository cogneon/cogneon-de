# About us

Cogneon GmbH was founded on 21 December 2001. We help companies, organizations and individuals succeed in the 21st century through the systematic use of knowledge. We do this through consulting, coaching, training and by providing open materials. Our main location is the Cogneon Akademie in Nuremberg, where we work and host trainings, workshops and community events.

## Vision

**Society** and **business** in the **21st century** are characterized by **constant change**. This is often described with the acronym [VUCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volatility,_uncertainty,_complexity_and_ambiguity) (volatility, uncertainty, complexity and ambiguity). **Individuals** and **organizations** of all kinds need to adapt to this through **continuous learning**. Otherwise they will not be **successful** in the future, and the **well-being** of all cannot be ensured.

In the past, many **good approaches** have been developed under labels such as [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management), [digital transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_transformation), [agility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agile_management), [further education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Further_education), [digital workplace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_workplace), [lifelong learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifelong_learning) and others. The next step is to **systematize** them and **bring** them **to a broad audience**.

On our website you can learn about our **vision of the learning society** and the role its most important actors play in it: **learning organizations**, **learning teams** and **lifelong learners** or **knowledge workers**.
