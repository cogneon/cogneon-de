gruppen:
  - id: strategisch
    titel: Strategic knowledge management
    text: For executives and managers who want to anchor knowledge management in their organization.
    karten:
      - titel: Knowledge Excellence Coach
        text: >-
          In workshops we define the direction of your knowledge management with you: scope, goals and measures. A coach then supports you during implementation, typically over one to three years.
      - titel: Knowledge advisory board
        text: >-
          An advisory board joins your management meetings and contributes the knowledge perspective. This way knowledge management becomes part of decisions instead of running alongside them.
      - titel: Knowledge Pioneers
        text: >-
          A group of organizations that want to implement knowledge management professionally and learn from each other along the way. Starting in 2027.
  - id: operativ
    titel: Operational knowledge management
    text: For teams and departments with a specific need.
    karten:
      - titel: Knowledge transfer
        text: Capture and pass on the experience-based knowledge of people, projects and teams.
        link: en/knowledge-transfer/
      - titel: Knowledge infrastructure
        text: >-
          Strategy, design and implementation of modern intranets, wikis, collaboration platforms and AI systems - built so that people use them in their daily work.
      - titel: Communities & Barcamps
        text: >-
          Knowledge exchange among equals in barcamps (on site, online or hybrid), knowledge networks and communities of practice. We help with design and roll-out.
      - titel: Learning Sessions
        text: >-
          45-minute learning sessions on knowledge management topics, available as an in-house format. The session catalog will be published in 2027 and will form the core of the online academy.
      - titel: Personal Knowledge Excellence
        text: >-
          A learning journey in six modules on personal knowledge and information management, with live input, exchange of experience and AI as a learning companion. Also available as an in-house format.
