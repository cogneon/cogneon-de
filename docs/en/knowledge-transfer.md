formate:
  titel: Three formats for three situations
  karten:
    - anlass: Someone changes roles or leaves
      titel: Expert Debriefing
      text: A structured interview process in which we capture a person's experience-based knowledge and hand it over to their successors.
      art: Interview process, since 1999
      link: en/expert-debriefing/
    - anlass: A project ends or reaches a milestone
      titel: Project Debriefing
      text: >-
        At the end of a project or at important milestones, the project team reviews its experience and records what the next project needs to know. Combines well with an offsite, for example at our Cogneon Akademie, so the team can also celebrate its successes.
      art: Workshop, on request
    - anlass: A team takes stock
      titel: Team Debriefing
      text: >-
        Typically as an annual review: the team makes visible who knows what and secures what is critical for its work. Combines well with an offsite, for example at our Cogneon Akademie, so the team can also celebrate its successes.
      art: Workshop, on request
