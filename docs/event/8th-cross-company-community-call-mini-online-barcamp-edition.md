# 8th Cross-Company Community Call – Mini Online Barcamp Edition

A mini online barcamp by & for Cross Company Community Managers ([#C3Managers](/tag/c3managers/) / [#C3Call](/tag/c3call/))! It’s a rare opportunity for best practice talk among peers without the buzz and noise of the big conferences!

Join us on **21st January 2021** , 4-5:30 PM CEST. And maybe even hold your own session!

**It’s a barcamp, so YOU can contribute actively!** All topics around communities and community management are welcome, especially (but not exclusively) when they are relevant for internal communities in organizations. The more interactive your session, the better. We and all participants are very happy about Q&As, joint brainstormings, discussions etc.!
