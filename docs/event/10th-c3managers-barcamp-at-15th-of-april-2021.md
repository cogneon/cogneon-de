# 10th C3Managers BarCamp at 15th of July 2021 w/ Richard Millington

The **10th Cross Company Community Managers Anniversary Meetup** (#C3Managers) will take place on 15th July 2021 , 4-5:30 PM CEST. We will host a mini online BARCAMP Edition combined with an inspirational keynote!

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/rich-millington.jpg)

*Richard Millington (Source: feverbee.com)*

Our Keynote Speaker for the anniversary is [Richard Millington](https://www.linkedin.com/in/richard-millington-5a32782/). Richard is the founder of [FeverBee](https://www.feverbee.com/), an international community consultancy, and the author of [Buzzing Communities](https://amzn.to/3qN7OXQ). Richard’s clients have included Google, Facebook, Oracle, Wikipedia, EMC, Greenpeace, and many more. Prior to FeverBee, Richard interned with Seth Godin in New York. Richard will give us an overview of his perspective on community management with a global perspective. He will talk about methods and frameworks as well as technologies and tools.
